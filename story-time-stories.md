# Part 1 – Stories

## How the stories work

The app has 10 stories across 3 levels, each 5 pages long. Every page is a picture, 1–2 sentences, and one activity.

| Level | What the child reads | Words per page | Page activity | End of story |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Level 1 | 3-letter words, one short vowel per story | 3–6 | Spell it: pick the missing middle letter | +15 stars, words found |
| Level 2 | 4-letter words with blends (fr, mp, ck) | 4–7 | Spell it: pick the missing blend | +15 stars, words found |
| Level 3 | 1–2 short sentences, sh / ch / magic e, ? and ! | 6–10 | Magic e, build the sentence, picture question, rhyme | +20 stars, Read / Understand / Spell stars |

**On every page, at every level:**

- **Read to me:** each word lights up as it is read.
- **Sound it out:** tap a word to split it into its sounds, then hear them blended.
- **Sight words:** these are shown in purple and read fast, not sounded out.
- **My turn:** the child reads the page aloud for +5 stars.

**Sight words used:** a, the, is, on, and, in, to, can, oh, no, she, so, he, said, look, up, they, we, you, was. Stories use only these, plus words the child can sound out at that level.

# Part 2 – Image prompts

**How to use these prompts**

- Make the character sheets for a story first. Use them as reference images (or reuse the same seed) for the cover and every page, so characters look the same in every picture.
- Paste the story's full style block where a prompt says `[style block]`.
- Sizes: pages 16:9 (1600 × 900, fits the app's story picture card), cover 1:1 (1024 × 1024).
- Keep the main action in the middle. The app's picture card crops a little from the edges.
